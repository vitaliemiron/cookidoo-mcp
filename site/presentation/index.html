01 / The question

What if “what’s for dinner?” didn’t begin with digital admin?

Cooking can be creative, healthy, comforting, or simply necessary. Copying data between recipes, calendars, and lists is none of those things.

02 / The friction

Inspiration lives everywhere. Execution lives in details.

A promising recipe can arrive from Cookidoo, another language, a health blog, or a screenshot. Making it usable means carrying intent through every handoff.

  1. Findthe idea
  2. Copythe full recipe
  3. Adaptlanguage and portions
  4. Structureguided actions
  5. Planthe right day
  6. Collectwhat to buy

03 / The principle

Automate the handoffs. Keep the human judgment.

The goal is not to remove choice from cooking. It is to remove the repeated copying, reformatting, and navigation that stands between a good idea and a meal.

05 / The outcomes

Time is only one thing automation can give back.

The same connected workflow can support healthier intentions, clearer instructions, and more room to try something unfamiliar.

TIME

Coordinate tasks without moving the same information by hand.

HEALTH

Intent becomes a plan

Move useful recipes into a visible week and ingredient list.

CLARITY

Steps teach as they guide

Separate ingredient handling from every machine action.

INSPIRATION

Ideas travel farther

Adapt recipes from other languages and sources.

07 / The responsibility

When an unofficial API changes, silence is not reliability.

Cookidoo can change without notice. The project runs authenticated contract tests every day, creates a regression issue on failure, and closes it after recovery.

monitoring rhythm, with encrypted CI credentials
