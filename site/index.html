Why it exists

A recipe idea should not become admin work.

The enjoyable part is choosing and cooking food. Copying details, translating steps, updating a calendar, and rebuilding a shopping list should not take over.

  1. 01 Ideas come from everywhere.Cookidoo, blogs, screenshots, friends, and other languages.
  2. 02 Useful recipes need clear steps.Especially when you are cooking something for the first time.
  3. 03 An idea is not yet a plan.The meal still needs a day and a complete ingredient list.
  4. 04 Connections can change.Daily checks help catch problems before they surprise you.

What it can do

Less copying. More cooking.

You keep the decisions. The assistant helps carry them from an idea into Cookidoo, your weekly plan, and your ingredient list.

01 / INSPIRATION

Turn “that looks good” into something you can actually cook.

Bring a recipe from Cookidoo or another source, translate it, and adapt it to your preferences.

02 / CLARITY

Make every step easier for a beginner.

Separate adding ingredients from mixing or cooking, so every action is easy to follow.

03 / HEALTH

Move healthy intentions into a practical week.

Turn good intentions into meals you can see on a real weekly plan.

04 / FREE TIME

Stop moving the same information from place to place.

Discuss, adapt, save, schedule, and collect ingredients from one conversation. Exact time saved depends on your routine.

05 / GUIDED COOKING

Keep Thermomix settings attached to the right step.

Time, temperature, speed, reverse, weighing, and supported cooking modes stay explicit.

06 / OWNERSHIP

Add the image that makes a personal recipe feel complete.

Add a photo you own, so a personal recipe is easy to recognize later.
