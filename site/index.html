The motivation

The kitchen had automation. The workflow around it didn’t.

Finding a promising recipe is easy. Turning it into the right language, clear Thermomix actions, a realistic week, and a usable shopping list is where the digital chores begin.

  1. 01 Inspiration is fragmented.Recipes arrive from Cookidoo, blogs, screenshots, and other languages.
  2. 02 Adaptation demands precision.Weights, time, temperature, speed, reverse direction, and modes must stay aligned.
  3. 03 Planning lives somewhere else.A recipe idea still has to become a calendar entry and shopping-list data.
  4. 04 Unofficial APIs change.Useful automation needs continuous tests, not wishful thinking.

What it gives back

Automation for the admin. Space for the human choices.

Cookidoo MCP does not decide what a good meal means to you. It connects the repetitive steps so you can focus on taste, health, curiosity, and the people at the table.

01 / INSPIRATION

Turn “that looks good” into something you can actually cook.

Copy official recipes for their full instructions, translate them, or adapt ideas from outside Cookidoo into a structured Thermomix workflow.

02 / CLARITY

Make every step easier for a beginner.

Keep weighing and adding separate from machine actions, with explicit settings and validated annotations.

03 / HEALTH

Move healthy intentions into a practical week.

Use recipe ideas, a seven-day planner, and shopping-list data as one connected planning surface.

04 / FREE TIME

Coordinate six digital tasks without six separate workflows.

Discover, copy, translate, structure, schedule, and collect ingredients from one AI conversation. Exact time saved depends on the meal and workflow; the repetitive handoffs are what the MCP removes.

05 / GUIDED COOKING

Preserve the details that make Thermomix guidance useful.

Model time, temperature, speed, reverse direction, weighing, Dough, Blend, Turbo, Steaming, Rice Cooker, Warm Up, and Browning.

06 / OWNERSHIP

Add the image that makes a personal recipe feel complete.

Normalize and upload an image you own through Cookidoo’s signed customer-recipe flow.

Trust by design

Useful automation needs visible boundaries.

Your credentials stay out of the repository.

Local use reads environment variables. CI uses encrypted GitHub Secrets, never committed values.

Recipe writes are structured and validated.

Pydantic models check annotation ranges, UTF-16 positions, and the separation between ingredients and machine actions.

External changes become actionable failures.

Five real API contracts run daily and open a regression issue when Cookidoo behavior changes.
